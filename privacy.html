Privacy

Charlie's First Year is a private diary for parents. Here is plainly what it keeps and who can see it. Last updated 17 October 2026.

What the app keeps

  • Your baby's name, birth date and whether they are a boy or a girl, if you enter them in Profile.
  • Feeds: the time and the amount of each one, and the bottle timer.
  • Nappies: the time, size and any note you add.
  • Moments: the photos and notes you add to a day.
  • Messages you send with "Send feedback".
  • A household code, and a random number that identifies each device. The app signs a device in without asking for an email address or a password.

What it does not do

  • No adverts, no tracking and no analytics.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone for marketing.
  • It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or microphone. Photos are only read from the picture you choose.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Everything is stored in a Google Firebase database, on Google's servers. Anyone who has your household code can see everything in that household, including photos, so share the code only with people you trust, such as a partner or grandparent. The person who runs the app can also see the database when fixing problems.

Everything the app needs to run, including its text style, is part of the app itself, so opening it does not contact any other company. Only your diary entries are sent to Google's Firebase database.

Keeping and deleting your data

Data stays until you ask for it to be deleted. Settings has a "Download a backup" button that saves a copy of everything to your device. To have a household deleted, email the address below with your household code and it will be removed.

Children

The app is for parents and carers, not for children to use. Information about the baby is entered by the parent who looks after them.

Changes

If this changes, the date at the top will change and the new version will be on this page.
