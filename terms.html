What the app is, and is not

Little sips is a diary for logging your baby's feeds, poos and moments, and sharing them with the people who look after your baby.

It is not medical advice. Expected feed times, totals and the patterns on the Trends page come from what you log. They are there to help you notice things, not to tell you what is right for your baby. Every baby is different. If you are worried about your baby's feeding, weight or health, speak to your health visitor, midwife, GP or NHS 111. In an emergency, call 999.
