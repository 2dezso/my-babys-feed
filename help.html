Logging feeds

  • A feed happening now: tap Start feed when it begins. When it is done, tap Finish feed and choose how much.
  • A feed you forgot: tap Log past and scroll to the time. If you do not know the amount yet, tap Just save the time, add the amount later.
  • Changing or deleting a feed: tap it in the list. Everything can be changed, and Delete this feed is at the bottom.
  • Next feed: when you finish a feed you can choose roughly when the next one is due. If it runs late, the app just shows how long ago it was expected. Every baby is different, so nothing is ever marked overdue.

Sharing with someone else

Everyone who feeds the baby can use the same diary on their own phone. There are no accounts. Instead each diary has a short code.

  1. On your phone, open Settings and tap Share code.
  2. On their phone, they open the app, tap I have a code from someone and enter the code.

Whatever either of you logs then shows on both phones straight away.

Anyone with the code can see and change the whole diary, including photos, so only share it with people you trust.

A new phone, or a lost one

Your diary is not stored only on your phone, so nothing is lost when you change phones, as long as you have the code. On the new phone, open the app, tap I have a code from someone and enter it. Everything comes back.

To keep a copy of the code, go to Settings and tap Save a copy of the code. You can send it to yourself, or save it in Notes. If someone else uses the diary, their phone also shows the code in Settings.

If every phone is lost and nobody kept the code, I cannot get the diary back for you. There are no accounts to reset.

Using it without signal

The app opens and works without signal, so night feeds in a room with no reception are fine. Anything you log is sent automatically when your phone is back online. Until then, other phones will not see it.

Backups

In Settings, tap Download a backup. Your phone saves a file with every feed, poo and moment. It includes your photos, so keep it somewhere private. It is worth doing now and then.

Settings and how the app looks

  • ml or oz: in Settings, under Feeding. Amounts are always saved in ml, so switching never changes your history, and each phone can show its own unit.
  • Colour: choose green, baby blue or rose in Settings.
  • Dark mode: the app follows your phone. Turn on Dark Mode in your phone's settings and the app switches with it.
  • On the home screen: in Safari on iPhone, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. The app then opens full screen like any other app.
  • Next feed reminders: these are only in the iPhone app from the App Store, because a website cannot send reliable notifications. They are set separately on each phone.

Is it medical advice?

No. Expected times, totals and trends come from what you log. They help you notice patterns, not tell you what is right for your baby. If you are worried about your baby's feeding, weight or health, speak to your health visitor, midwife, GP or NHS 111. In an emergency, call 999.
